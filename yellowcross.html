    Goal: Make the Yellow Cross

    The last layer consists of 4 yellow edge pieces and 4 yellow corners. The goal in this step is to orient the yellow edges so that their yellow sticker is facing the top layer. After this step, only the yellow corners need to be oriented and then all that is left is to permute them.

    Step 1: Identify the shape

    Case 1: The "L"

    In this case 2 adjacent edges with their yellow stickers are facing up and the two others arent' resulting in a shape that looks like an "L". Now, imagine the yellow side is a clock. the yellow edge at the back is 12 o' clock and turning clockwise, add 3 hours for each edge. (the right one 3 o' clock, the front one 6 and the left one 9) Turn the top layer until the 2 edges with their yellow sticker facing up are at 9 o' clock and 12 o' clock.

    Case 2: The Line

    In this case 2 opposite edges with their yellow stickers are facing up and the two others arent' resulting in a line. Turn the top layer until the line is horizontal

    Case 3: The Dot

    In this case none of the edge's yellow stickers are facing up, resulting in a "dot" There is no specific way to hold the cube here, but keep in mind that the yellow side should always be on top.

    Step 2: Performing the algorithm

    Now that you have identified the shape and oriented the cube correctly, you now want to perform the following algorithm:

    Cross Algorithm: F R U R' U' F'

    🎉 Yellow cross done — only orienting the yellow corners and permuting the last layer needs to be done!

    Common Mistakes

    • Rotating the entire cube instead of only the top layer
    • forgetting to put the 2 edges at 9-12 in case 1 of step 2
