    • Beginner Guide — Complete Your First Layer

    Before You Start

    • Daisy is complete
    • Yellow center is on top
    • White edges surround the yellow center

    If your Daisy isn’t ready yet, it’s okay — go back and review it first.

    Goal: The First Layer

    The first layer is the bottom face fully white, with all side colors matching their centers.

    Step 1: Turn the Daisy into the White Cross

    Match Colors

    Turn the top layer until the side color of a white edge matches the center below it.

    Move Edge Down

    Once matched, rotate that face twice to move the white edge to the bottom.

    Repeat

    Do this for all four white edges.

    ✅ White cross on the bottom, side colors aligned

    Step 2: Add the White Corners

    Find the Correct Corner (Top Layer)

    Look for a corner with white + two matching side colors that is on the top layer.

    Position It Above Its Spot (Top Layer)

    Hold the cube so the white cross is on the bottom. Find where the corner belongs on the bottom layer. Turn the top layer only so the corner is directly above its target position. Rotate the cube itself if needed so the corner is in the front-right position of the top layer — this is the position it must be in before executing the algorithm. Keep the white cross on the bottom — only the top layer moves for alignment.

    Move the Corner Down (Top Layer)

    Execute the sequence R U R' U' to move the corner from the top layer into the bottom layer. Repeat as needed until the corner drops into place. Do this for all four corners.

    Bottom Layer Case

    Sometimes the corner you need is already in the bottom layer but not in the correct position:

    • Hold the cube so the white cross is on the bottom.
    • Rotate the cube itself so the corner is in the front-right position of the bottom layer.
    • Execute the algorithm R U R' U'. This moves the corner to the top layer.
    • Then follow the top layer steps to place it correctly.

    Repeat Until First Layer is Complete

    Handle each corner one at a time. After all corners are in place, the entire first layer is solved:

    • White face is solid
    • Side colors match centers

    🎉 First layer complete — white face and side colors aligned!

    Common Mistakes

    • Corner twisted incorrectly
    • White edge or corner broken after placement
    • Cube looks scrambled in the process

    Don’t worry — mistakes are normal. Check and try again as needed.
