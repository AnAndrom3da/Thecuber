    Goal: Permute the Yellow Corners

    The Second-to-Last Step to solve the cube is to permute the yellow corners. The goal in this step is to permute the yellow corners so that they are in their respective spot without breaking the rest. After this step, all that is left is to permute the yellow edges.

    Step 1: Identify the case

    Case 1: Diagonal

    In this case the solved corners are diagonal from each other. Turn the top layer until the solved corners are top right and bottom left.

    Case 2: Not Diagonal

    In this case the solved corners aren't diagonal from each other. Turn the top layer until the solved corners are top left and bottom left.

    Step 2: Performing the algorithm

    Now that you have identified the case and oriented the cube correctly, you now want to perform one of the following algorithm depending on the case you have:

    Case 1 (Diagonal) Algorithm: (F R U' R' U' R U R' F') (R U R' U' R' F R F')

    Case 2 (Not Diagonal) Algorithm: (R U R' U' R' F) (R2 U' R' U' R U R' F')

    🎉 Yellow corners are in place done — only one more step to go!

    Common Mistakes

    • Rotating the entire cube instead of only the top layer
    • Accidently doing the opposite move like R instead of R' or the opposite
