    Goal: Learn the OLL basics

    The algorithms you learned in the beginner's method are simple but not the fastest ones and not that efficient . Before learning all 57 OLL algorithms you want to learn the basics to make your life easier later on.

    Step 1: Identify the case (Cross)

    The cases for the yellow cross in this method are the same then the beginner's method. The only difference is the way we are going to position the cube and later on the algoithm too.

    Case 1: The "L"

    In this case, instead of positioning the 2 edges at 9 and 12, position them at 3 and 6.

    Case 2: The Line

    In this case, nothing changes from beginner to intermediate.

    Case 3: The Dot

    In this case, nothing changes from beginner to intermediate.

    Step 2: Performing the algorithm

    Now that you have identified the case and oriented the cube correctly, you now want to perform one of the following algorithm depending on the case you have:

    Case 1 (The "L") Algorithm: f (Sexy) f'

    Case 2 (The Line) Algorithm: F (sexy) F'

    Case 3 (The Dot) Algorithm: (case 2) (Case 1)

    🎉 Great! You are half way there! Keep going!

    Step 3: Identify the case (corners)

    The cases for the yellow corners (or yellow side) in this method are the same then the beginner's method. The only difference is the way we are going to position the cube and later on the algoithm too.

    Case 1: The "Fish" or "Sune"

    In this case, nothing changes from beginner to intermediate.

    Case 2: The other "Fish" or "Anti-Sune"

    In this case, nothing changes from beginner to intermediate.

    Case 3: The "H" or "Double-Sune"

    In this case, nothing changes from beginner to intermediate.

    Case 4: The "π" (Pi) or "T-Shirt"

    In this case, nothing changes from beginner to intermediate.

    Case 5: The "U" or "Headlights"

    In this case, position the cube just like in the beginner's method and do a U'.

    Case 6: The "T" or "Hammerhead"

    In this case, nothing changes from beginner to intermediate.

    Case 7: The "L" (Not the Same as for Yellow Cross!) or "Triple-Sune"

    In this case, position the cube just like in the beginner's method and do a U2.

    Step 4: Performing the Algorithm

    Now that you've identified the shape and oriented the cube correctly, it's time to perform one of the algorithms depending on the case you’ve got. The "Fish" algorithm is used for all cases, but in a specific order and with a few changes.

    Case 1 (Sune): (R U R') U (R U2 R')

    Case 2 (Anti-Sune): R U2 R' U' R U' R'

    Case 3 (H): (R U R') U (R U' R' U) R (U2 R')

    Case 4 (Pi): (Sune) U' (Sune)

    Case 5 (Headlights): R2 D' R U2 R' D R U2 R

    Case 6 (Hammerhead): r U R' U' r' F R F'

    Case 7 (L): l' U R D' R' U' R D x'

    🎉 Nice! You have now learned the OLL basics! Now next step → the PLL basics
