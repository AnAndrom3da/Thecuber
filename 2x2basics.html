    Welcome to the 2x2 Cube

    The 2x2 cube is often called the "Pocket Cube".

    Unlike the 3x3, it has no edge pieces and no center pieces. It is made up entirely of corner pieces.

    How Is 2x2 Different From 3x3?

    • No center pieces
    • No edge pieces
    • Only 8 corner pieces total
    • Each piece has 3 colors
    Think of the 2x2 as just the corners of a 3x3 cube.

    Your Goal

    The goal is to make each side one solid color.

    Because there are only corners, solving the 2x2 is faster but requires learning how corner pieces move.

    Step 1: Solve One Layer

    Start by solving one full face (usually white).

    Make sure the bottom layer is fully solved before moving on.

    Step 2: Solve the Top Layer

    Once the first layer is complete, you will use a simple algorithm to orient and permute the remaining corners.

    Basic Beginner Algorithm

    R U R' U'

    This is the most important beginner algorithm for 2x2. You will use it multiple times to solve the cube.

    You’re Ready

    The 2x2 is a great cube to start speed solving.

    Practice solving slowly first — speed comes later.
