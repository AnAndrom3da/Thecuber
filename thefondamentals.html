    • Intermediate Guide — The Fondamentals

    Goal: Learn the fondamental algorithms

    Great cubers aren’t built on shortcuts — they’re built on fundamentals. Before moving on to OLL and PLL, you must be completely comfortable with these core triggers. They are the building blocks of high-level solving. Train them until they feel automatic. Because the stronger your foundation, the faster you rise. “The more we sweat in practice, the less we bleed in battle.” — Muhammad Ali

    Sexy move: R U R' U'

    Left sexy: L' U' L U

    Inverse sexy move: R U' R' U

    Inverse left sexy: L' U L U'

    Sledgehammer: R' F R F'

    Hedgehammer: F R' F' R

    Right insert: R U R'

    Left insert: L' U' L

    Sune: R U R' U R U2 R'

    Anti-sune: R U2 R' U' R U' R'

    T-perm: (R U R' U') R' F R2 (U' R' U') (R U R') F'

    Ua-perm: (R U R') U R' U' R2 U' R' (U R' U R)

    Ub-perm: (L' U' L) U' L U L2 U L (U' L U' L')

    They might look tricky, but once you understand how the pieces move and where they go, you’ll master them quickly — skip this, and you’ll struggle later; conquer it, and you’ll move faster than most cubers ever will. “Don’t watch the clock; do what it does. Keep going.” — Sam Levenson
