    Goal: Solve the Second Layer

    The second (middle) layer consists of the four edge pieces that do not contain yellow. The goal is to place each edge so that the side colors match their centers , while keeping the first layer solved . After this step, only the top (yellow) layer will remain unsolved.

    Step 1: Identify a Middle Layer Edge

    Find an edge piece with no yellow on it. Check if it is already in the middle layer and in the correct spot. If it is misplaced, it will need to be removed first (covered later). Always keep the white cross on the bottom while working.

    Step 2: Insert an Edge from the Top Layer

    Align the Edge Above Its Center

    Hold the cube so the white cross is on the bottom . Find the edge piece in the top layer. Turn the top layer only until the edge’s front color matches the center it sits above .
    Example: edge is green-orange, with orange facing up . Turn the top layer so it sits above the green center . This ensures it goes into the correct slot when you apply the algorithm.

    Check Direction: Left or Right

    Look at the top color of the edge (the color on the top face):

    • If it should go to the right , use the right algorithm.
    • If it should go to the left , use the left algorithm.

    Edge Goes to the Right

    Algorithm: U R U' R' U' F' U F Beginner Explanation: Keep the white cross on the bottom . Move the top layer until the edge is in front, then follow the moves step by step. The edge will move into the middle layer on the right.

    Edge Goes to the Left

    Algorithm: U' L' U L U F U' F' Beginner Explanation: Keep the white cross on the bottom . Move the top layer until the edge is in front, then follow the moves step by step. The edge will move into the middle layer on the left.

    Misplaced Edge Already in Middle Layer

    If an edge is already in the middle layer but in the wrong place:

    • Move it out to the top layer first using one of the algorithms.
    • Then insert it correctly using the top layer instructions above.

    Repeat for All Edges

    Solve all four middle layer edges, one at a time. Keep the white cross on the bottom and align each edge above its correct center before inserting.

    🎉 First two layers complete — only the yellow top layer remains unsolved!

    Common Mistakes

    • Rotating the entire cube instead of only the top layer
    • Misplacing edges and breaking the first layer
    • Not checking which direction (left or right) the edge should go
