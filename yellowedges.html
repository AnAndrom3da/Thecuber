    Goal: Permute the Yellow Edges

    The Last Step to solve the cube is to permute the yellow edges. The goal in this step is to permute the yellow edges so that they are in their respective spot without breaking the rest. After this step, you are done!

    Step 1: Identify the case

    Case 1: Clockwise

    In this case there is a solved edge and 3 unsolved edges. Turn the top layer until the solved edge is in the back. If the edges need to move clockwise, then you're in the right case. If they need to turn counterclockwise, then you've got case 2.

    Case 2: Counterclockwise

    This case is identical to the previous one but with a little twist. Turn the top layer until the solved edge is in the back. If the edges need to move counterclockwise, then you're in the right case. If they need to turn clockwise, then you've got case 1.

    Case 3: Opposite

    In this case there are no solved edges For this case, there isn't any way to hold the cube. the only important thing is that the white side should be down (yellow top).

    Case 4: Adjacent

    This case is identical to the previous one but with a little twist (again...). For this case, turn the top layer until the yellow edge of the side you're facingis the same color as the corners to your left

    Step 2: Performing the algorithm

    Now that you have identified the case and oriented the cube correctly, you now want to perform one of the following algorithm depending on the case you have:

    Case 1 (clockwise) Algorithm: R2 (U R U) (R' U' R' U' R') U R'

    Case 2 (Counterclockwise) Algorithm: L2 (U' L' U') (L U L U L) U' L

    Case 3 (Opposite) Algorithm: (Case 1) U' (Case 1) U

    Case 2 (Adjacentl) Algorithm: (Case 1) U (Case 1) U'

    🎉 Good Job! You have solved your first 3x3 cube!

    Now all that's left is to learn these steps and algorithm and you'll be able to solve the cubeon your own.

    Once you're done make sure to check the intermediate level!
