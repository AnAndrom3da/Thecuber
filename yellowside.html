    Goal: Solve the Yellow Side

    Now that you have oriented the yellow edges correctly, you want to solve the yellow side. The goal in this step is to orient the yellow corners so that their yellow sticker is facing the top layer. After this step, permuting the yellow corners and edges is all that's left to finish the cube.

    Step 1: Identify the shape

    There are a total of 7 possible cases in this step:

    Case 1: The "Fish" or "Sune"

    In this case, there is only 1 corner's yellow sticker facing up, resulting in a shape that looks like a "Fish". In this case, turn the top layer until the yellow sticker on the corner is placed in the front-left position of the cube. Looking at the side you're facing (with white on the bottom), if the top-right sticker is yellow, you're in the right case. If not, you're in Case 2 .

    Case 2: The other "Fish" or "Anti-Sune"

    This case is identical to the "Fish" case but with a slight twist. In this case, turn the top layer until the corner is placed in the back-right position. Looking at the side you're facing (with white on the bottom), if the top-left sticker is yellow, you're in the right case . If not, you're in Case 1 .

    Case 3: The "H" or "Double-Sune"

    In this case, there are no corners oriented correctly. Turn the top layer until the top-left and top-right corners of the side you're currently facing (with white on the bottom) are yellow. Rotate the entire cube 180° to check that the top-left and top-right corners of the side you’re now facing are also yellow. If they are, you're in the right case . If not, you're in Case 4 . Now do either a U or U'.

    Case 4: The "π" (Pi) or "T-Shirt"

    This case is identical to the "H" case but with a slight twist (again). Turn the top layer until the top-left and top-right corners of the side you're currently facing (with white on the bottom) are yellow. Rotate the entire cube 180° to check that the top-left and top-right corners of the side you're now facing are not yellow. If they aren't yellow, you're in the right case . If they are yellow, you're in Case 3 . Now, turn the top layer so that only the front-top-right corner of the side you're facing is yellow.

    Case 5: The "U" or "Headlights"

    In this case, there are 2 corners oriented correctly. Turn the top layer until the solved corners are placed in the front-left and front-right positions . Now, turn the entire cube 180°. Looking at the side you are currently facing (with white on the bottom), if the top-left and top-right stickers are yellow , you’re in the right case . If not, you're in Case 6 . To fix this, turn the entire cube again so that the front-top-right and back-right corners are both yellow.

    Case 6: The "T" or "Hammerhead"

    This case is identical to the "U" case but with a slight twist. Turn the top layer until the solved corners are placed in the front-left and front-right positions . Now, turn the entire cube 180°. Looking at the side you are currently facing (with white on the bottom), if the top-left and top-right stickers are both not yellow , you're in the right case . If they are yellow, you're in Case 5 . Now, turn the top layer so that only the top-left corner of the side you are currently facing is yellow.

    Case 7: The "L" (Not the Same as for Yellow Cross!) or "Triple-Sune"

    In this case, there are also 2 corners oriented correctly. Turn the top layer until the solved corners are placed in the back-left and front-right positions .

    Step 2: Performing the Algorithm

    Now that you've identified the shape and oriented the cube correctly, it's time to perform one of the algorithms depending on the case you’ve got. The "Fish" algorithm is used for all cases, but in a specific order and with a few changes.

    Fish Algorithm: R U R' U R U2 R'

    Case 1 (Fish): R U R' U R U2 R'

    Case 2 (Anti-Sune): (Fish) U 2*(Fish)

    Case 3 (Double-Sune): 2*(Fish)

    Case 4 (Pi): (Fish) U' (Fish)

    Case 5 (Headlights): (Fish) U (case 4)

    Case 6 (Hammerhead): (Fish) U' (Case 2)

    Case 7 (Triple-Sune): 3*(Fish)

    🎉 Yellow side complete — only permuting the pieces needs to be done!

    Common Mistakes

    • Rotating the entire cube instead of only the top layer
    • Not checking the shape and orienting the top layer correctly
